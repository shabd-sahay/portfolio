Research Experience

  • 2025 – now

    IIIT Bhopal

    FedVC: Version-controlled governance of client contributions in federated learning
    Under review
    Federated Learning, Byzantine-Robust Aggregation, Anomaly Detection
    • Built a governance layer that keeps an unmodified FedAvg server inside a Git-style repository: trusted aggregates are committed to main, each suspected client gets a side branch, the best commit is restored at fixed intervals, and isolated clients receive a verdict every cycle.
    • Four modules: an adaptive commit gate, graduated trust zoning, a checkpoint restoration scheduler and a verdict classifier.
    • With 100 clients (30 label-flippers) on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and EMNIST-Balanced, cut the poisoned share of aggregation weight from 0.144–0.227 under nine baselines (including Krum, Multi-Krum, FLTrust and FoolsGold) to 0.009–0.022: a 10–16× reduction.
    • Kept attackers out of 83–89% of rounds where every baseline admitted them in all rounds, and banned all of them on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST without banning an ordinary honest client, while matching undefended FedAvg's MCC and uploading 18–24% less data.
    • Measured and reported the failure cases: under strongly non-IID data (Dirichlet α = 0.1) the poisoned share stayed at 1.5–2.9% vs 15–22% for FedAvg, but many honest clients were also banned; sign-flip attackers were contained rather than banned.

    Malware detection under label scarcity, quantized for CPU
    Semi-Supervised Learning · Gradient Boosting · INT8 Quantization · ONNX Runtime · Streamlit
    • Built a static-PE malware detector on EMBER2018 (2,381-dim features, 25% of training rows unlabeled), scoring detection as TPR at a fixed 1% false-positive rate rather than accuracy; reached 0.865 TPR at full supervision and 0.936 AUC from only 566 labels.
    • Benchmarked 8 semi-supervised configurations across 3 seeds; none beat supervised training. Isolated the cause as confidence-based selection bias: pseudo-labels were 99.8% correct yet still degraded the decision boundary.
    • Diagnosed an INT8 regression that collapsed detection from 0.789 to 0.178 (activation outliers at ±992σ), fixed via input clipping touching 0.08% of values. The final ONNX INT8 model is 3.95× smaller and 3.6× faster (0.109 → 0.030 ms p50) at ≤0.008 TPR loss.
  • 2026

    Live demo

    Synthetic scenario augmentation for demand forecasting
    Time-Series Forecasting · Gaussian Mixture Models · Synthetic Data Augmentation · LLM Evaluation
    • Built a three-way comparison testing whether AI-generated synthetic demand scenarios improve retail forecasting, on 307,645 sales rows across 34,053 products, using a chronological, leakage-free train/test split.
    • Designed a Gaussian Mixture Model trained on 111,682 real month-to-month sales ratios (component count chosen via BIC) to generate synthetic scenarios; matched the real demand distribution 7× more closely than a hand-written baseline (KS statistic 0.030 vs. 0.201).
    • Ran 5-seed Wilcoxon testing across hand-written, LLM and self-trained generators; neither significantly improved accuracy over real data (p = 0.13, p = 0.44), a null result.
